隐私政策

生效日期：2026 年 8 月 13 日 · 适用于 iOS 应用「萌天天」（Meng Tiantian）

欢迎使用「萌天天」（以下简称「本应用」）。我们重视您的隐私。本政策说明本应用如何收集、使用、存储与保护信息。使用本应用即表示您已阅读并理解本政策。

1. 我们是谁

本应用由萌天天开发团队提供，Bundle ID 为 com.tiatian.meng。如对本政策有疑问，请通过文末联系方式与我们沟通。

2. 我们收集哪些信息

本应用无需注册或登录。我们不会要求您创建账户，也不会主动收集姓名、手机号、邮箱等可直接识别个人身份的注册信息。

根据您使用的功能，本应用可能在本机处理以下信息：

  • 创作内容：绘画、填色、贴纸场景、拼图等作品与相关进度。
  • 学习数据：算术练习记录、正确率、错题、每日任务完成情况、成就与设置项。
  • 故事相关内容：阅读进度、您主动录制的语音朗读（如使用录音功能）。
  • 设备权限相关数据（仅在您授权后使用）：
    • 相机：用于扫描纸质故事书页面并识别文字，以提供点读/朗读；影像仅用于当前扫描，不会自动上传。
    • 麦克风：用于录制故事页面的朗读声音，便于在「我的录音」中回放；录音默认保存在本机。
    • 相册：在拼图等功能中，您可选择导入本机图片。

3. 信息如何存储与传输

  • 作品、录音、练习进度与设置等主要内容默认保存在您的设备本地。
  • 我们不会将上述创作与学习内容作为常规业务流程自动上传到我们的服务器。
  • 卸载应用或清除应用数据可能导致本地内容不可恢复，请自行备份重要内容（如系统允许）。

4. 广告与第三方服务

本应用可能集成第三方广告或商业化相关 SDK（例如穿山甲 / 优量汇等）。在展示广告或进行广告效果统计时，第三方可能根据其自身隐私政策，收集设备标识符、粗略位置、广告互动数据等非精确个人信息，用于广告投放、防作弊与效果衡量。

我们不会向广告合作方出售您的本地创作内容或录音。第三方如何处理数据，以其届时有效的隐私政策为准。您也可在系统设置中管理广告跟踪相关选项（视 iOS 版本与系统能力而定）。

5. 我们如何使用信息

  • 提供画画、故事、算术练习等核心功能；
  • 保存您的作品、进度与成就；
  • 在您确认后，可能调用系统评分接口以收集应用评价反馈；
  • 在启用广告时，用于展示广告与衡量广告效果；
  • 改进稳定性与用户体验（例如排查崩溃，若后续接入诊断工具将另行说明）。

6. 信息共享

除以下情形外，我们不会向第三方出售或出租您的个人信息：

  • 获得您的明确同意；
  • 为提供广告等服务所必需，且仅在最小必要范围内与服务商共享；
  • 根据法律法规、诉讼、政府主管部门的强制性要求。

7. 数据安全

我们采取合理的技术与管理措施保护本地存储与应用安全。但请理解，任何互联网或电子设备都无法保证绝对安全。请妥善保管设备并及时更新系统与应用。

8. 儿童与未成年人

本应用面向广泛用户。若由监护人协助未成年人使用本应用，请监护人指导权限授权与内容管理。我们不会故意在未获监护人同意的情况下收集儿童的可识别个人信息。

9. 您的选择

  • 可在系统设置中随时关闭相机、麦克风、相册等权限；关闭后相关功能可能不可用。
  • 可在应用内管理学习相关设置，并删除本机保存的作品或录音（以应用内提供的能力为准）。
  • 可通过卸载应用停止使用本服务并删除本机应用数据。

10. 政策更新

我们可能适时更新本政策。更新后的版本将在本页面发布，并更新「生效日期」。重大变更时，我们将通过应用内提示或其他合理方式告知。

11. 联系我们

如对本隐私政策或个人信息处理有任何问题、意见或请求，请通过以下方式联系：

  • 电子邮箱：Geetikagujral@icloud.com
  • 或通过 App Store 产品页的「App 支持」渠道联系开发者

我们将在合理期限内予以回复。

Welcome to Meng Tiantian (the “App”). We respect your privacy. This Policy explains how information is collected, used, stored, and protected. By using the App, you acknowledge that you have read and understood this Policy.

1. Who we are

The App is provided by the Meng Tiantian team. Bundle ID: com.tiatian.meng. For questions about this Policy, contact us using the details at the end of this page.

2. Information we process

The App does not require registration or sign-in. We do not ask you to create an account, and we do not proactively collect registration details such as name, phone number, or email.

Depending on the features you use, the App may process the following information on your device:

  • Creations: drawings, coloring pages, sticker scenes, puzzles, and related progress.
  • Learning data: arithmetic practice records, accuracy, review items, daily missions, achievements, and settings.
  • Story-related content: reading progress and voice recordings you choose to make.
  • Permission-related data (only after you grant access):
    • Camera: used to scan printed story pages and recognize text for read-aloud features. Images are used for the current scan and are not uploaded automatically.
    • Microphone: used to record narration for story pages so it can be replayed in My Recording. Recordings stay on the device by default.
    • Photo library: you may import local images for features such as puzzles.

3. Storage and transmission

  • Creations, recordings, practice progress, and settings are stored locally on your device by default.
  • We do not automatically upload your creations or learning content to our servers as part of normal operation.
  • Uninstalling the App or clearing app data may permanently remove local content. Please back up important items when possible.

4. Advertising and third-party services

The App may integrate third-party advertising or monetization SDKs (for example, industry ad networks in China). When ads are shown or measured, those partners may collect device identifiers, coarse location, and ad interaction data under their own privacy policies for delivery, fraud prevention, and measurement.

We do not sell your on-device creations or recordings to advertising partners. How third parties process data is governed by their policies. You may also manage tracking-related options in system settings, depending on your iOS version.

5. How we use information

  • To provide drawing, stories, math practice, and related features;
  • To save your creations, progress, and achievements;
  • After your confirmation, to request App Store ratings via the system API;
  • When ads are enabled, to display and measure advertising;
  • To improve stability and experience (diagnostic tools, if added later, will be disclosed).

6. Sharing

We do not sell or rent your personal information except:

  • with your clear consent;
  • as needed to provide advertising or similar services, limited to what is necessary;
  • when required by law, legal process, or competent authorities.

7. Security

We take reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect the App and local storage. No method of electronic storage is perfectly secure. Please keep your device secure and keep the system and App updated.

8. Minors

The App is intended for a general audience. If a guardian assists a minor in using the App, the guardian should guide permission grants and content management. We do not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children without appropriate consent.

9. Your choices

  • You may revoke camera, microphone, or photo access in system settings; related features may stop working.
  • You may manage learning settings in the App and delete local creations or recordings where the App provides that option.
  • You may uninstall the App to stop using the service and remove local app data.

10. Changes

We may update this Policy from time to time. The updated version will be posted on this page with a revised effective date. For material changes, we will provide notice in the App or by other reasonable means.

11. Contact

For questions about this Policy or our data practices:

  • Email: Geetikagujral@icloud.com
  • Or contact the developer via App Support on the App Store product page

We will respond within a reasonable period.
